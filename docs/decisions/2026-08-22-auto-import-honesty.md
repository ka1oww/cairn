# The import promise — 22 August 2026

**The app promises only what the platform will let it keep.**

iOS never wakes a third-party app because a new photo appeared in the library.
There is no background trigger for it, and there is no entitlement that buys
one. Any wording that implies photos flow into the trip on their own is a
promise the platform will not let the app keep.

**So the app says what is true.** The import runs when Cairn is open. The
wording in the interface commits to that and nothing more, and the moment the
app opens it does the catch-up sweep so the gap between truth and expectation
is as small as it can be.

**Why this matters more than it looks.** The failure mode of the optimistic
wording is not a mild disappointment. It is a person who believes their photos
are in the trip, does not open the app for three days, and finds a hole in the
record that they cannot tell was ever their responsibility to fill. Honest
wording puts the one action that is needed - open the app - where the person
can see it, which is the only version where the record ends up complete.
