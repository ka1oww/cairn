# The book's treatment — 23 August 2026

The book's visual character, re-asked after authored words reopened it in
[book round nine](2026-08-22-book-round-nine.md), and now closed. The answer
is **the blend**: round nine's `17d` is the settled treatment, not a
recommendation.

## The three directions

Round nine drew the same day of the book three ways, and the choice settles
the character of the app's final artefact:

- **A. Admit it is generated.** Clean, machine-made, no pretence.
- **B. Simulate the handmade.** Scrapbook character the app never earned.
- **C. The blend (`17d`).** Generated, but built only out of what the app
  genuinely already owns: the tilt that is already in the capture, the dashed
  thread, and a page wash tinted from that day's own photographs.

The captain answered the blend directly on the board: *"yes."*

## Why it is the honest one

What kills B is that a handmade look records labour nobody performed. A tilt
applied by a renderer is a costume. The blend survives that test because
every one of its handmade-feeling elements is a fact the app is already
holding: the photograph really is tilted, the thread really does connect the
day's prints in order, and the page wash is derived from the colours of the
photographs actually on that page. Nothing is invented to look old.

A and B are kept here rather than dropped, because the argument between them
is what produced the answer, and the next person who wants to add a torn
edge or a coffee ring will need it.

## The rule

State it as a rule, not a style, because it decides later cases:

> **The book may only look handmade in ways that are true.**

Any ornament proposed for the book has to name the real fact it is drawing.
If it cannot, it is B, and it is out — however good it looks.

## What this does not settle

How an **authored line** is set on the page. Round ten reopened that as a
separate question (`18e`), because a caption is labour a person really did
perform — which is exactly what this rule permits, and why the register is
a live question rather than a closed one. Answering the treatment does not
answer it; it is on the round-ten board.
