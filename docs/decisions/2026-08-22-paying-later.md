# Pay Apple only when the friends' phones need it — 22 August 2026

Decided by the captain, on the answer board:

> Just want to build the app first and then only at the last part that we start
> paying right because you told me that we have seven days to play with the app
> before I have to start paying money

## The decision

**Defer the $99/year Apple Developer Program.** Build and test on the
captain's own iPhone using a free Apple ID for as long as that works.

## The seven days he is remembering, stated precisely

A free Apple ID can sign an app onto **your own device**, but the provisioning
profile **expires after seven days** — after which the app stops launching
until it is re-signed and reinstalled from Xcode. A free account is also
capped at three signed apps at a time. That is a real, workable development
loop for one person on one phone, and it costs nothing.

## Where the deferral actually ends, which is earlier than "the last part"

The free path signs onto **your own** device only. The moment the app has to
reach **anyone else's** phone it needs TestFlight, and TestFlight needs the
paid program. In the current plan that moment is the **weekend test with
friends** at the end of the sync-and-ping phase — the gate before any real
trip, and roughly two thirds of the way through the work, not at the end of
it.

So the decision holds, with its trigger named honestly: **pay at the weekend
test, not at the finish line.** Discovering this on the weekend itself would
cost the weekend.
