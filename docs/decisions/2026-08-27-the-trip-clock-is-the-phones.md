# The former phone clock and sync standing — 27 August 2026

> Superseded for trip-clock behaviour by [the destination clock decision](2026-09-08-the-trip-clock-is-the-destination.md).

This record preserves the 27 August work that made `SyncStanding.awaitingTripRow`
visible and allowed an unnamed trip to publish under its wire placeholder. Its
former decision to use the phone's IANA zone is no longer current: a new shared
trip now needs an explicit destination zone, and an unknown zone remains local
and quiet. The destination-clock decision is the sole authority for those rules.

## Historical context

A build once supplied no timezone at all, so it could not create a shared row
and the app did not show the reason. The standing and its person-facing copy
remain; the missing destination zone is now one of the facts that can honestly
produce that standing. The unnamed-trip and status-copy decisions remain in
force; the trip sheet and Trail tell the person when a local plan is waiting
for the information needed to share it.
