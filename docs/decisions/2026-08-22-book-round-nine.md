# Book round nine — 22 August 2026

Two answers from the round-nine review board, on the book's interior drawn
against the captain's printed reference.

## The cover's face — the photograph, with the cairn signing the foot

Surface 17a drew the photograph dominant in a wide cream border, with the
cairn reduced to a small pressed mark at the foot. The captain, on that
surface: *"actually this is cuter."*

This settles a genuine tension rather than ignoring it.
[Design calls](2026-08-22-design-calls.md) §6 gave the cover to the cairn as
the trip's portrait, and surface 13e drew that answer. 17a keeps the cairn on
the cover but has it sign the book rather than front it. The photograph is
the face.

## People may write their own words into the book — yes

Captain: *"yes sure of course, why not its cute and funny."*

Every word in the round-nine spreads is derived: stop names from the plan,
credits from who was present, seals from a formula. Authored captions are now
permitted alongside them.

Consequence, and it is larger than the question looked. This changes the
generated-versus-handmade fork rather than sitting beside it. Round nine
argued that simulated handwriting is dishonest because a generated book has
no human labour to record. Authored words are exactly that labour. So the
book can now tell the truth in two registers: what the app derived, set in
type; what a person actually wrote, in their own hand. That distinction is
honest, and it is warmer than either pole was.

The treatment fork is therefore reopened rather than closed, and goes back to
the captain in the next round with this synthesis on the table.

What authored words do *not* imply is a book editor — that implication was
caught and refused the same day, in
[the book is automatic](2026-08-22-book-no-editor.md).
