# The alert level — 22 August 2026

**The ping does not break through Do Not Disturb.**

The ping is registered as an ordinary local notification. It is not a
time-sensitive or critical alert, and it does not request the entitlement that
would let it pierce a Focus mode or escape the scheduled notification summary.

**Why.** Cairn is a companion to a holiday, not a system that has any claim on
someone's attention. A person who has turned their phone to Do Not Disturb on
holiday has made exactly the choice the app should respect. The cost of
honouring that is one missed slot, and the app already handles a missed slot
gracefully: the late-photo path stays open, and a day with a gap is still a
real day. The cost of not honouring it is an app that overrides a person's
stated wish in order to ask them for a photo, which is the wrong trade for
something this small.

There is also a practical edge. The time-sensitive entitlement is reviewable at
submission, and a reviewer who reads "reminds you to take a photo" is entitled
to reject it. Asking for a permission that is both wrong and refusable is a
bad bargain twice over.
