# December is the target, and there is no trip yet — 22 August 2026

Decided by the captain, on the list board, asked whether a real trip is in the
calendar:

> not really. i think maybe december

## The answer

**No trip is in the calendar.** December is the working target, chosen rather
than discovered — which is the right way to handle it, because the alternative
was not "no deadline", it was "the work expands until something else stops
it".

Treat this as a provisional anchor, not a commitment. If a real trip appears
with a real date, that date replaces this one and scope bends to fit it.

## The two things that would spend the slack

1. **The printed page has never been designed.** It blocks the book only,
   needs no code, and can be drawn in parallel starting any time. Left to the
   end it becomes the critical path.
2. **Nothing has been shown to any of the eight.** A late discovery that
   something obvious is wrong is exactly what six weeks of slack exists to
   survive — but only if the discovery happens early enough to use it.
