The book interior.

The printed reference arrived — five photographs of a real couples’ photo book, handmade: stickers, pins, tilted polaroids, handwriting. Three things stop it transferring whole: Cairn’s book is generated, it is for eight people with no protagonist, and every layout must survive however many photos a day produced — two or nine. So this round drew the same day twice with the same eight photographs and settled the central fork by looking, then drew the rest of the book around the answer. Everything is grounded in docs/decisions/: hours prominent, names at timestamp weight, no fixed grid and no empty seats, late photos at their real hour, the cairn placed exactly as decided. Digital only — no bleed, no spine, no print geometry.

MY RECOMMENDATION

Neither pole. A’s honesty, warmed only by what the app already owns — drawn below as 17d. Looking at both: B is charming at arm’s length and hollow up close, because the charm of the reference is the labour it records, and a generated book has no labour to record. But pure A overshoots — it strips out tilt and thread that are not simulation at all: every print in Cairn’s own screens already sits at ±2° on a dashed thread. So the blend makes exactly three moves, none invented: the app’s tilt, the app’s dashed thread as the timeline, and a faint page wash tinted from the day’s own prints — the same honest derivation the cairn’s stones already use. A warm day yields a warm page because the day was warm, not because a template said so. The book reads as the day artefact grown up, which is what it is.

Open questions — flagged, not answered

1. The cover’s face: photo or pile? Decision 6 makes the cairn the trip’s portrait — “the book’s cover, the spine on the shelf, the share image” — and 13e drew the cover that way. The reference’s cover (05) is one photo in a wide border, and this round’s brief points there. 17a draws the photo dominant with the cairn as a small pressed mark, which honours both readings but decides neither. A needs-decision line is on the status file.

2. Can anyone write into the book? Every word in these spreads is derived — star names and stops from the Trail, credits from presence, seals from a formula. The reference’s warmth is substantially its handwriting, and the record neither grants nor forbids people writing captions of their own (at binding night, say). If authored words are ever allowed, they are real labour and deserve real weight — that decision would soften the A/B fork itself. Also flagged needs-decision.
